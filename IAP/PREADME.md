# Introducción al Aprendizaje Profundo (Introduction to Deep Learning)

This folder contains the projects completed during the Deep Learning course, focusing on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs).

1.  **Angaut_Práctico_1_Aplicación_de_NLP.ipynb** – Implemented a comprehensive Sentiment Analysis pipeline on the IMDB dataset. We established a baseline using sparse representations (TF-IDF) with classical classifiers (Logistic Regression, XGBoost) and compared them against dense vector embeddings (Word2Vec, FastText) and Transformer-based models (BERT). The project focused on benchmarking traditional NLP techniques against context-aware embeddings.

2.  **Angaut_Práctico_2_Aplicación_de_LLMs.ipynb** – Explored the capabilities of Small Language Models (SLMs) for text classification, specifically using the Qwen family (< 1B parameters). We configured the model for deterministic generation to act as a chatbot and evaluated its performance using Zero-shot and Few-shot prompting strategies. Finally, we compared the inference quality of the generative model against the supervised models trained in Project 1.
